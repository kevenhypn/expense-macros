Expense Macros

Support

Install on iPhone

In Safari, open Expense Macros, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen and turn on Open as Web App. Open it once online to prepare the offline version.

Where is my data?

Data stays in the app or browser you use. The Home Screen app and native installs do not share or sync entries.

Report a problem

Use the GitHub issue tracker. Include your device and what you were trying to do. Keep your personal financial details out of the report.
